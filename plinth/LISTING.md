# Plinth — listing copy

## Title

Plinth — Premium Editorial Shopify Theme | Modern Minimal Ecommerce Template

## Short description

A premium Shopify 2.0 theme for brands that want their products to look
considered. Editorial layouts, a hairline product grid, slide-out cart,
instant search, and a design system you rebrand from nine colours.

## Long description

**Plinth is not a mockup. It is a working Shopify theme.**

Upload the zip, and your real products, prices, variants, inventory and
checkout are already running. Shopify does the commerce. Plinth does the
design.

---

### The look

Most Shopify themes present products as floating shadowed cards in a grid.
Plinth divides them with hairlines on a continuous ledger — the way a printed
catalogue sets an index. Numbered sections. Tabular figures. Generous margins.
One accent colour, used sparingly.

The result reads as a considered brand rather than a store built from a
template. Which is the point.

---

### Change everything in five minutes

- **Four palette presets** — Ink & Chalk, Midnight, Bone & Clay, Slate & Citrus
  — or set your own nine colours.
- **Two type personalities** — Editorial (display serif) or Modern (grotesk).
  One dropdown, two completely different-feeling stores.
- Page width, spacing, corner radius, button shape, card style, badges — all
  controls, no code.

### Who it is for

Founders and small studios doing real revenue on Shopify who have outgrown a
free theme, cannot justify a few thousand for a custom build, and care that
their store looks like someone made a decision.

The niche is intentionally open. Nothing assumes a category — ceramics, denim,
audio, coffee, skincare, furniture, jewellery all sit comfortably in it.
