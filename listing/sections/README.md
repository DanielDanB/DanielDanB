# Section panels

Eight listing images, one per section of the site, in the laptop + phone layout.
The hero panel is not here — that one already exists.

All are 3000 × 2000 px (3:2), which fits Etsy, Gumroad, Creative Market and a
website hero alike.

| File | Headline | Bottom caption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sec-about.png` | Your story. One clean block. | Written by you. Laid out for you. |
| `sec-services.png` | Any trade. Your own icons. | One template. Every trade. |
| `sec-process.png` | Six steps. Or three. Or ten. | How you work, step by step. |
| `sec-materials.png` | Swipeable gradient cards. | Options, one swipe apart. |
| `sec-portfolio.png` | Your work, with no frame. | The work, front and centre. |
| `sec-testimonials.png` | Proof, in their words. | What your clients say. |
| `sec-faq.png` | Answers that open on tap. | Questions, answered up front. |
| `sec-contact.png` | Enquiries, straight to you. | Where the visit becomes a job. |

Each panel carries the same furniture: the ABC LAB lockup, a two-line headline
with the second line in the accent colour, a short paragraph, four features with
icon tiles, and a highlight pill — then the section itself running on a laptop
and a phone.

Every screen is a real capture of `abclab-mockup.html`, scrolled so the section
heading sits just under the sticky navbar, exactly as a visitor sees it. Two
things are settled before the shutter: the Google Maps embed cannot load without
network access, so it is swapped for a drawn placeholder, and the process
timeline is opened in full — its cards open one at a time on scroll, and a still
of the section top would otherwise show the later steps as empty stubs.
